BOUCLIER v3.0 - Local redaction studio
Created by Polaether - https://polaether.polaether.workers.dev/

CONCEPT
Bouclier hides sensitive data (names, addresses, e-mails, phone numbers, IBANs, cards, ID numbers, faces) in your images, texts and files. It also cleans metadata (EXIF, GPS, author) and helps you spot scam messages.

PRIVACY
- All processing happens on your computer.
- No network calls during normal use: your documents never leave your PC.
- No telemetry, no account, no ads.
- Only exception: the optional OCR setup (Tesseract) may download a component, and only if you ask for it.
